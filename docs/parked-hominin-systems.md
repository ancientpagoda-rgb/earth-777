# Parked hominin systems

Earth 777 currently keeps only the aggregate hominin lineage/population-index model active in the Free Earth runtime.

The following prototype modules remain in the repository but are deliberately dormant:

- `HomininDemography.js`
- `HomininDemographicInheritance.js`
- `DemographyTelemetry.js`
- `HomininSocialOrganization.js`
- `HomininWaterTransport.js`
- `HomininConflictConstruction.js`
- `render/DemographyReadout.js`

They were parked because explicit headcounts, migrating demes, households, sites, storage, exchange, water transport, conflict, resource seizure, and defensive construction are ahead of the current hominin evidence pipeline and had become coupled to surface presentation that has since been removed.

Reactivation should happen only after ROAD/ROCEEH-style hominin evidence envelopes are integrated and the runtime has a clear evidence-constrained spatial population representation. At that point, reintroduce advanced behavior incrementally rather than booting the entire prototype stack at once.
